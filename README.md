Emergency Power Station – Single Line Diagram (Metro Rail Line 9 Extension)
Overview

Open-ended lab project for EEE 316L – Engineering Drawing & Electrical Services Design Lab (Independent University, Bangladesh). The task placed the student in the role of an Electrical Design Engineer responsible for designing the Single Line Diagram (SLD) of an emergency power station for a metro rail line extension, built entirely in AutoCAD Electrical.

Task Brief

"Suppose you are an Electrical Design Engineer at a reputed engineering firm. The firm has got a project to setup an emergency Power Station for the extension of Metro Rail Line 9, and you will be responsible for designing and drawing Single Line Diagram of an emergency Power Station in AutoCAD Electrical. Note: The Metro Rail runs on 1100 Volts. So, make sure 1100 volts are fed into the Metro rail feeder to run the trains."

Design Summary

Two power sources feed a common 1100V metro rail bus through an Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS): the 11kV utility grid, stepped down via transformer, and a diesel generator providing backup. From the 1100V bus, two rectifier-fed feeders supply the up-track and down-track sections.

Design Notes

This is coursework, not a certified engineering deliverable. Component sizing (transformer/generator capacity relative to combined feeder demand) and the Normal/Emergency source assignment on the ATS reflect the assignment as designed and drawn, and haven't been independently verified against a production substation design standard.
